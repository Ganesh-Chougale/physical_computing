`GP`: General Purpose.  
`GND`: Ground.  
`ADC`: Analog-to-Digital Converter.  
`PWM`: Pulse Width Modulation.  
`SPIO SCK`: Serial Peripheral Interface 0, Serial Clock. 
`I2C1 SDA`: Inter-Integrated Circuit 1, Serial Data.   
`I2C1 SCL`: Inter-Integrated Circuit 1, Serial Clock.  
`SPIO TX`: Serial Peripheral Interface 0, Transmit / MOSI.
